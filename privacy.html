Privacy

Last updated: September 2026. This notice describes how V1.0 of YIANG HEALTH handles information.

Client-side Processing

The Journey Planner in V1.0 runs primarily in your browser. Your planning inputs (country, age range, purpose, duration, budget, optional free text) are processed locally to generate a mock plan. They are not automatically uploaded to a server in this version.

What We Ask You Not to Enter

  • Medical records or detailed clinical history
  • Passport or national ID numbers
  • Bank or payment card details
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Emergency medical information that should go to emergency services instead

Local Storage

We may store your language preference in the browser’s localStorage so the interface remembers your choice. You can clear this at any time via browser settings.

No Accounts in V1.0

There is no user registration, login, or cloud account system in this version. Future versions may introduce accounts (email, Google, Apple, WeChat, etc.) with appropriate privacy notices at that time.

Analytics & Logs

V1.0 does not include third-party analytics SDKs by default. If hosting providers collect standard server logs (IP, user-agent), those are subject to the host’s policies.

Future Changes

When cloud sync, accounts, payments, or real AI APIs are introduced, this privacy notice will be updated before those features process personal data on servers.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the official contact channel published on the site when available.

Safety Privacy Terms

Yiang Health provides information organization and journey planning services only. Not medical advice. Not emergency service.
