WHY SHANXI

Not just a stop on the map.

Shanxi's appeal lies in the connections between landscape, historic architecture, folk traditions, food and the rhythm of local life.

Ancient architecture

Temple halls, courtyard compounds and historic settlements offer a window into regional building traditions. Check opening hours and conservation rules before visiting.

Mountain landscapes

The Taihang range and its valleys invite scenic drives, seasonal walks and slower rural stays. Weather and trail access vary by season.

Living local culture

Folk performance, regional cuisine, traditional crafts and village life can turn a checklist trip into a deeper cultural encounter.
